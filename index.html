Pick a flight

Click any airplane on the globe to see its flight, its airline, and the aircraft's maker and specs. Its 3D model appears next to the globe, at the same scale as every other type.

Drag to turn the globe, scroll or pinch to zoom. M switches globe and flat map, T starts a tour.

The traffic is simulated: real airlines, routes and aircraft types, but the positions are generated, not live ADS-B tracking. Specs are the makers' typical published figures; real aircraft vary by variant and cabin layout.

Map: Natural Earth via world-atlas. Glasses-free 3D with DisplayXR inline-3D and three.js.
